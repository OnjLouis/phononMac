Getting Started

Copy Phonon for Mac from the disk image into Applications, open it and wait for Ready. Choose a language and voice, then use Test Voice. Refresh Voices updates the system list; it may take a short time to appear.

The preview sample follows the selected language. You can type your own text without losing it when you change languages. Reset Sample restores the example for the selected language.

In VoiceOver Utility, add a Phonon voice to your speech voices and select it. Keep a familiar Apple voice available as an alternative. Closing this application does not disable the voices.

The first system utterance can take several seconds while Core ML prepares the engine. Subsequent utterances stream without waiting for the whole text.

Imported Voices

Choose Manage Imported Voices, then Add Voice to open a compatible .safetensors file. Name the voice and choose its language. Phonon keeps a local copy and leaves your original file alone. Select an import and choose Remove Voice to delete the copy. VoiceOver Utility may retain a blank saved entry after removal; remove that entry in VoiceOver Utility if it remains.

Speech Controls

VoiceOver supplies rate, pitch and volume. Phonon generates streaming audio; Sonic adjusts rate and pitch during generation. Navigating cancels obsolete speech.

Languages

English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Voices are grouped by their published locale. English character voices have their own group in this app and appear under English (United States) in VoiceOver. They speak English; this grouping does not claim that every character has a US accent.

Updates

Check for updates offers Never, Hourly and Daily. Daily is the default. Checks run when this settings app opens and at the selected interval while it stays open; no login item or update helper runs after you quit. Check Now performs a manual check.

Updates require a valid Phonon signature, matching download size and hash, the correct Apple developer signature and notarization. Installation closes and reopens this app, preserving its preferences and prepared model data. VoiceOver can continue speaking through its loaded voice; restart VoiceOver when convenient after an update. Do not interrupt installation.

Privacy

Speech stays on this Mac. No spoken text or audio is saved by the app. The settings app contacts GitHub to check for updates and download an update you approve. It sends no speech text. Automatic checks can be disabled.
