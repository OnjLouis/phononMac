Phonon for Mac 1.0.0

Speech is generated on your Mac without an Ollama server.

What's New

Version 1.0.0: native, offline VoiceOver speech in five languages with 102 voices; streaming rate, pitch and volume controls; voice preview and signed updates.

Getting Started

Open Phonon for Mac and wait for Ready. Choose a language and voice, then use Test Voice. Refresh Voices updates the system list; it may take a short time to appear.

In VoiceOver Utility, add a Phonon voice to your speech voices and select it. Keep a familiar Apple voice available as an alternative. Closing this application does not disable the voices.

The first system utterance can take several seconds while Core ML prepares the engine. Subsequent utterances stream without waiting for the whole text.

Speech Controls

VoiceOver supplies rate, pitch and volume. Phonon generates streaming audio; Sonic adjusts rate and pitch during generation. Navigating cancels obsolete speech.

Languages

English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Voices are grouped by their published locale. English character voices have their own group.

Updates

Check for updates offers Never, Hourly and Daily. Daily is the default. Checks run when this settings app opens and at the selected interval while it stays open; no login item or update helper runs after you quit. Check Now performs a manual check.

Updates require a valid Phonon signature, matching download size and hash, the correct Apple developer signature and notarization. Installation closes and reopens this app, preserving its preferences and prepared model data. VoiceOver can continue speaking through its loaded voice; restart VoiceOver when convenient after an update. Do not interrupt installation.

Privacy

Speech stays on this Mac. No spoken text or audio is saved by the app. The settings app contacts 3.onj.me to check for updates and download an update you approve. It sends no speech text. Automatic checks can be disabled.
